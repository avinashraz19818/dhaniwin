v28 me kya fix hua (since v27)
------------------------------
WIN/LOSS POPUP: timer khatam hone ke baad ab win/loss popup aayega.
Pehle popup kabhi khul hi nahi raha tha.

Root cause: popup ka logic (js/BetRule-DyIqbhnc.js me getWinLossResult)
ye check karta tha ki bet wali period history ki SABSE TOP row par hai
ya nahi. Is site ke engine me timer khatam hote hi NAYI period top par
aa jati hai, isliye bet wali period index-1 par chali jati thi -> purana
check ("> 0") bet ko "purana" samajh kar delete kar deta tha aur API call
kabhi hoti hi nahi thi -> popup kabhi nahi aata tha.

Fix (3 cheezein):
1. Check ko engine ke hisaab se adjust kiya: ab index 0 ya 1 dono par
   popup trigger hota hai (matlab "abhi-abhi khatam hui round"). 2+
   round purani bet par popup skip hi hota hai (wahi purana safety).
2. Popup lock: reveal-animation aur auto-refresh timer dono ek saath
   check karte the -> duplicate popup ka race tha. Ab ek result par
   sirf ek popup.
3. Lagatar 2 round par bet lagane par ab dono ka popup unke apne timer
   end par aata hai (pehle dusri bet ka record clear ho jata tha).

Backend (Lottery/GetWinLossResult) already sahi result de raha tha -
status/winAmount turant milta hai (instant settle). Koi backend change
is version me nahi hai.

Pichhle sab fixes bhi isi pack me hain:
- v27: period numbering "1 period piche" (owner pasand) + timer end par
  history me result apne aap aana
- v26: login fixes (migrations order, PHP8 fatal, MySQL fallback log)
- v25: Wingo balance consistency
